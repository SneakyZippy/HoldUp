Privacy Policy

100% Offline • Zero Tracking • Privacy First
Application: HoldUp (com.sneakyzippy.holdup)
Last Updated: November 2026
Developer: SneakyZippy

Our Privacy Promise: HoldUp is an open-source, local-first Android application designed to help you break compulsive phone habits. HoldUp does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal data. The app does not request Android internet permission and makes zero network calls.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because HoldUp operates strictly on your device without network capabilities, it is physically impossible for user data to leave your phone:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The app contains zero analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, or Sentry).
  • No Advertisements: HoldUp is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising tracking libraries.
  • No Account Required: All functionality is available immediately without any account registration or login.

2. Device Permissions & Usage

HoldUp requires specific Android permissions to detect when shielded apps open and to present mindful pauses. Here is exactly how each permission is used:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
AccessibilityService Detects foreground app launches to trigger your chosen mindful pause before you start doomscrolling. Also performs the standard Home gesture when you choose "Walk Away". Zero Logging. Only the active package name is compared against your local shielded list. HoldUp never reads, inspects, or logs keystrokes, passwords, chats, or screen contents (canRetrieveWindowContent="false").
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
(Usage Access)		 Displays today's accumulated screen time on the reality check banner to give you conscious context when opening an app. Queried locally in real-time. Usage metrics are never transmitted or stored persistently.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Allows the mindful pause activity to appear cleanly over the launched app. Visual overlay display only. No screen recording or background capture.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers a gentle soft reminder notification when your intended mindful session timer (5, 10, or 15m) expires. Scheduled and delivered locally on your device.
VIBRATE Powers rhythmic tactile haptic feedback during guided breathing pauses. Local hardware actuation only.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE Maintains an active timer notification during an allowed session so you are gently notified when time runs out. Local system notification lifecycle only.

3. AccessibilityService API Policy Compliance

In accordance with Google Play's Accessibility policy:

  • HoldUp uses Android's AccessibilityService API strictly for core habit-friction features: detecting foreground app launches and automating the "Home" navigation gesture upon walking away.
  • HoldUp provides a prominent in-app disclosure before requesting this permission.
  • HoldUp never uses accessibility access to record audio, capture user credentials, alter system settings without permission, or bypass security boundaries.

4. Local Storage & Data Retention

All configuration data (such as your shielded apps, custom reflection quotes, mindful points, and chosen accent theme) is stored strictly in Android's private DataStore sandbox on your device.

Uninstalling HoldUp immediately and completely deletes all stored data.
